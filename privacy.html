Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Purpose

Personal Rclone Drive is a private, personal-use application that uses rclone to manage authorized Google Drive file transfers and backups.

Information Accessed

The application accesses Google Drive files and metadata only in accounts explicitly authorized by the owner.

How Information Is Used

Google Drive data is used for personal backup, archival, and file-transfer operations.

Data Sharing

The owner does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers. Files are transferred only between authorized storage accounts.

Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials are kept in the owner's private rclone configuration. This website does not collect or store Google Drive files or authentication credentials.

Data Retention

Archived files are retained according to the owner's backup and retention preferences.

Access Revocation

Google account access can be revoked through the Google Account security settings.

Contact

For questions, use the support email displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
